# Transfer photos from iPhone to MacBook

1. On the iPhone, select the photos and videos in **Photos**, tap **Share → Options**, choose **Automatic**, and turn on **All Photos Data**. Then AirDrop them to the MacBook. This sends the original files with available metadata and edit history.
2. On the MacBook, keep the received files together. An item may arrive as a folder containing several related files, such as `.HEIC`, `.AAE`, and, for a Live Photo, `.MOV`. Import the complete folder into **Photos** rather than importing only the `.HEIC` file.
3. Check the imported items in Mac Photos: confirm the edited appearance, date and location, Live Photo playback, and whether **Revert to Original** works for an edited photo.
4. If **iCloud Photos** is enabled for the Mac Photos library and the iPhone, the imported items can sync back to the iPhone through iCloud. Confirm they appear correctly on the iPhone before relying on this round trip. Keep a separate copy of the AirDropped folders outside the synced Photos library before deleting anything: deleting an item from iCloud Photos on one device deletes it from the other devices too.

An average iCloud storage use of **7.45 MB per photo** can be normal. For example, **2.4 GB ÷ 322 photos ≈ 7.45 MB per photo**. Actual sizes vary with resolution, Live Photos, edits, and format.

See [Apple's iPhone sharing guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/share-photos-and-videos-iphf28f17237/27/ios/27) for **All Photos Data**.
